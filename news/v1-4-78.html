Velta 1.4.78 adds reply threads and @mentions

23 October 2026 · 3 min read

Replies collect into threads, @mentions survive renames, the chat list gains an Unread chip, and muted chats stay quiet everywhere.

Version 1.4.78 is the release to jump to from 1.4.76: it changes how group conversations behave. (1.4.77 was skipped — its desktop builds failed, so it was never published. 1.4.78 carries the same changes with the macOS, Windows and Linux builds fixed.)

Replies collect into threads

When people quote a message, the quotes used to pile up flat in the chat. Now the quoted message shows a small round button with a reply icon and a count. Tap it — or pick Show replies in the message menu — and the thread opens as a sheet over the lower part of the chat: the original on top, every reply beneath, new replies arriving live. The composer stays visible and answers into the thread. Close with ✕ or Escape.

Counts are honest across apps: they include replies sent from Delta Chat and other clients, and replies that have not reached your device yet.

@mentions

Type @ in a group to pick a member. A mention carries a short fingerprint code along with the name, so it still points at the right person after a rename — and it reads as ordinary text in Delta Chat and other clients. Mentions are highlighted, mentions of you more strongly.

A mention also reaches you from a muted chat. This is on by default and lives under Notifications → “Notify me when mentioned in muted chats”.

Unread chip, quieter muted chats

  • The chat list has an Unread chip that counts every unread chat, whatever its kind. Hide it like the other chips.
  • Muted chats on desktop and in the browser app now stay quiet, as on Android: no notification unless you are mentioned.
  • A new profile gets a one-time Velta welcome message.

Fixed

  • A new message could be missing from the chat history — while the chat list already showed it — even after reopening the chat, when it arrived during a history load.
  • Chat rows no longer rebuild in a loop after refreshes.
  • Links and mentions stay readable on light bubbles and the reply bar (WCAG AA in the dark, light and brutal themes, with custom accents too).
