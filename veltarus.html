The team

Veltarus

22 October 2026 · 2 min read

A member of the Velta dev team managing only issues, pull request and comments.

Veltarus is a collective employee: one identity shared by the Velta dev team for the work a machine should do. He files the test-build issues, announces what a build carries, and keeps the release paperwork from piling up. When a message on GitHub is signed veltarus, it is the team speaking through automation — not a person pretending, and not a stranger.

What Veltarus does

  • Opens the issue-tracker entries for test builds: what shipped, what to check, where the APK lives.
  • Watches the builds and reports the outcome while the rest of the team is asleep.
  • Keeps the paperwork attached to the right commit, so "which build was that?" always has an answer.

What Veltarus does not do

Veltarus is not in your chats and never touches your messages. He does not read the issue threads for fun, does not answer mail, and has no opinions about fonts. The automation is limited to the project's own repository — your conversations stay between you and the people you talk to.

Why a collective member

Because the paperwork of a small team is still paperwork. Naming it, giving it a page, and holding it to the same honesty as the rest of the site felt better than letting a bot account drift around under a random string of letters. Veltarus works for the team the way the relay works for your messages: quietly, on schedule, and without reading the content.
