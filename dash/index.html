Velta Dash

Files sent straight between Velta apps — direct when both ends are online, your chatmail relay only when they are not, sealed at rest, resumable, with an honest email fallback.

What it is

Files over 6 MB between two Velta apps go direct: an iroh QUIC path straight device-to-device when both ends are online. The only relay ever used is the sender's own chatmail server — the relay it advertises — never a third party and never n0. Under 6 MB rides as a normal mail attachment.

The transfer card

The file row lives in the message bubble: name, size, a Dash badge, and live progress that fills to 100% in place. On Android the row verbs are Save and Share; on desktop, Show in folder; in the PWA, Save. Retry resumes the partial instead of starting over. And the card offers Ask for the email copy when the fetch cannot be completed.

Sealed and resumable

  • The sender's spooled copy is sealed at rest under the sender's key (VLTA sidecar).
  • The download is sealed under the receiver's key.
  • An interrupted stream resumes from the .part instead of starting over.
  • Free space is checked before a byte is written; the hash is verified at the end.
  • Cancel/withdraw deletes the stored copy.

The honest fallback

If the fetch fails, is unreachable, or the ticket expires, the card offers the email copy: the sender mails the file once on request (a VXFER-RESEND ticket), not silently. One resend per ticket.

Limits

  • 2 GB ceiling, hard. The card says 2 GB; older Velta releases refuse anything larger outright, so a bigger file never starts.
  • The web app / PWA cannot Dash. iroh is QUIC/UDP — no wasm target, no beacon, sandboxed files. The card falls back to asking for the email copy.
  • Both ends must be Velta that proved it. The Chat-Velta-Xfer capability header on a real message; a peer that hasn't proven it gets the email fallback path, not a silent "sent".
  • Direct needs both online. With no direct addresses (CGNAT) the bytes go through the sender's chatmail relay — still no third-party relay.
  • Over 2 GB is also too big for email. The answer is "both online, sender resends".
  • Folder sends are zipped on the device, STORE-only entries (deflate entries are refused, not copied as garbage). The zip pre-flight warns above 512 MB and refuses above the engine limit.
  • Ticket life is 24 h by default. An unfetched offer is mailed once from the sender's stored copy; a delivered offer is withdrawn. After a profile is recreated, stored copies are revalidated so a "Received" card can't linger over a dead file.

What Delta Chat problems it solves

Big files in plain Delta Chat ride as mail attachments (provider size caps, the copy sits on the relay/mailbox). There is no direct device-to-device path for large files. "Sent" is not "received". And the old classicTransfer webxdc is a fake-UI holdover.

Dash gives a direct path when both are online, a sealed spool on the sender device only, a real 100% progress bar, a card that explains itself to Delta Chat users (and hides the explainer for Velta users), Android cap parity, and an honest failure line instead of a success label over a dead fetch.
